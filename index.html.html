Open questions

What we need answered

HighCan it be built in three weeks?

A hinged metal box with a card lock is custom work. If not, the fallback is a 3D-printed body with a metallic finish and magnetic handles that release when the card comes out. It looks and feels the same.

HighBrand sign-off on the shape

Happy Meal is a kids' brand and the film is dark. We use only the silhouette, no name or logo, but brand should confirm this is fine.

MediumNo faces on the product

Putting SRK's face on it needs a separate likeness approval. The design avoids it on purpose and uses film symbols instead.

MediumHow much "blood"

The drips are stylised enamel, not gore. Easy to tone down to plain gloss red if brand safety asks.
